# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not welcome. Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate this, and may ban repeat offenders.

Report concerns by opening a private security advisory on this repository or contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
